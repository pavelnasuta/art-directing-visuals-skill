# Art-directing visuals

Use `skills/art-directing-visuals/SKILL.md` as the canonical workflow for posters, covers, banners, thumbnails, and social graphics. Do not use it for full UI or brand-system work.

Before generating, establish the message, audience, channel, required copy, format, facts, and rights constraints. Choose one subject-specific concept. Never invent facts or copy protected work. Verify exact copy, dates, numbers, contrast, mobile readability, artifacts, and usage rights before delivery.
